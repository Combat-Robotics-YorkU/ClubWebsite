Sponsors

Help us build what's next.

We want to keep the club as accessible as possible, so anyone can build and fight robots at little to no cost, while still competing at a high level. Sponsors make that possible.

Become a sponsorSee sponsorship tiers

Where your money goes

Every dollar ends up in a robot.

  • Parts and materials

    Motors, controllers, ESCs and raw materials. On a robot this small, the costs add up fast.

  • Competitions

    Entry fees and travel to get our robots to events.

  • Tools and fabrication

    3D printing, filament, tools, CNC and machining.

  • Testing and safety

    Combat robots get destroyed in testing, and replacing parts is expensive. We also won't cut corners on safety gear or a proper test box.

What you get

Why sponsor us.

Get seen

Your logo on our robots, team shirts, this website and every match video we post.

Work with the team

We're a brand-new team, so the parts and tools we start with become our standard. Get your product into our builds early and it grows with the club.

Hire from us

Meet students who have designed, built and repaired real hardware under a deadline.
