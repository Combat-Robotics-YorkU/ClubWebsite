About us

Small robots. Serious engineering.

We are a student design team at the Lassonde School of Engineering that builds combat robots and takes them to competition. Our goal is to give students real, hands-on engineering experience that classes can't.

Our mission

Combat robotics is one of the fastest ways to learn engineering. A robot has to be light, powerful and tough enough to survive a weapon designed to destroy it. Every fight shows you exactly what worked and what didn't.

We want any student, in any program, to be able to design a part, build a robot and drive it in a real arena. We focus on the 1 lb and 3 lb classes, where robots are affordable to build, quick to rebuild and safe to test on campus.

How a fight works.

Two robots enter an enclosed, polycarbonate-walled arena. The fight ends with a knockout, or after three minutes the judges score damage, aggression and control.

Match length
3 min
Robots per fight
1 v 1
Or a judges' decision
KO

Team structure

Four subteams. One robot.

Members join a subteam, but every robot is built by all four together.

Mechanical

Chassis, armour, weapons and drivetrains. CAD, stress analysis, machining and 3D printing.

Electrical

Batteries, motor controllers, brushless weapon motors and wiring that survives a direct hit.

Software

Radio control, motor tuning, telemetry and the tools we use to test and simulate designs.

Business

Sponsorships, finances, events, social media and keeping the team running.
